Entry for [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf)
on make.fun — one line, 200 characters at most, in English: the elevator
pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup.

## The pitch (197 characters)

> Investors: the city drops 40 million crumbs a day and nobody cashes in. My delivery fleet already lives on every rooftop — and works for breadcrumbs. I'm raising one seed round. Literally one seed.

## Why this line

- **A real pitch, not just a bird joke:** problem (40 million crumbs a day,
  nobody cashing in), solution (a delivery fleet already living on every
  rooftop), ask (one seed round).
- **The joke lands twice:** the fleet "works for breadcrumbs" — no hiring,
  no depots, no wages in money — and the closer, "Literally one seed",
  because a pigeon's seed round is, of course, a seed.
- **Only a pigeon could say it:** rooftops, breadcrumbs, seeds — the voice
  and the city life stay in view the whole line.
- **Tight:** 197 of the 200 characters, every word earning its place.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the pitch to
`/output/entry.txt`. Outside the grader, `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stands in
for `/output`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
